mysql> help show tables

Description:
Syntax:
SHOW [EXTENDED] [FULL] TABLES
    [{FROM | IN} db_name]
    [LIKE 'pattern' | WHERE expr]
SHOW TABLES [FROM db_name]

+---------------+
